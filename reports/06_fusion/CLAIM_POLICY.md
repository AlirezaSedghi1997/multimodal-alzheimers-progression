POST_HOC_INTERNAL_EXPLORATORY

Completion describes an executed audit, not clinical efficacy. Outcomes are future sustained CDR progression; they are not a diagnosis of Alzheimer disease. Prior post-all maps and positive tau claims are not primary evidence until their replacements pass the recorded checks. All prespecified baselines and failures are retained.
