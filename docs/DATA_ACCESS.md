# Data availability and confidentiality

The study used restricted A4/LEARN data. Obtain access through the provider and
follow the applicable approved data-use application and agreement:
https://www.a4studydata.org/dua

The published agreement limits redistribution outside permitted uses, requires
team compliance, and addresses confidentiality of data and weights used by AI tools.
This package does not establish what the project owner's signed agreement or a
particular recipient permits. A private GitHub repository or private Drive link
is not in itself redistribution permission. No participant information is sent to
an external search service by the packaging workflow.

Raw/processed individual images, identifiers, individual dates, predictions,
embeddings, membership tables and weights are excluded. Recipients must supply
restricted inputs via an authorized process. Face-containing MRI and individual
attribution overlays are not distribution figures. Template and atlas licenses
must be checked independently; publicly downloadable is not automatically reusable.

For manuscripts, use the provider's required acknowledgement and citation from
the applicable terms. Those obligations are not waived by private distribution.
